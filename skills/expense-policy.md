Fernbrook Co. expense policy, version 7. These rules are internal and specific to
Fernbrook; do not guess them from general knowledge.

Meals
- The daily cap for all meals combined is $37, per person.
- Team dinners are capped at $29 per person and the receipt must show the headcount.
- Alcohol is never reimbursable.

Flights
- Book at least 14 days before departure. Later bookings need approval code LB-3.
- Economy only. Flights over 6 hours may use premium economy with code PE-2.

Hotels
- Nightly cap: $164 in Boston, Denver and Austin; $118 everywhere else.
- Stays over 5 nights need a written reason from the traveler.

Ground transport
- Rideshare is capped at $45 per trip.
- Rental cars always need code RC-9.

Approvals
- Any single expense over $500 needs approval code ZX-4 from a director.
- Anything over $2,000 needs two codes: ZX-4 and ZX-9.

Receipts and timing
- A receipt is required for anything over $12.
- Submit within 21 days. Late submissions are reimbursed at 80%.

Exceptions
- Only the Finance Lead can grant exceptions, in writing, before the spend.
